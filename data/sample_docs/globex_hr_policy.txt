GLOBEX INC — REMOTE WORK POLICY

Effective Date: March 1, 2025

Overview
Globex Inc supports flexible remote work arrangements for eligible employees. This policy outlines expectations, eligibility, and equipment provisions.

Eligibility
Employees who have completed 90 days of employment and maintain satisfactory performance reviews are eligible to request remote work arrangements. Managers approve requests on a case-by-case basis.

Equipment
Globex will provide a laptop, monitor, and a one-time home office stipend of $500. Employees are responsible for maintaining a secure and reliable internet connection.

Working Hours
Remote employees are expected to be available during core hours of 10am-3pm in their local timezone, and must attend all scheduled team meetings.

Security Requirements
All remote employees must use company-issued VPN software when accessing internal systems. Personal devices may not be used to access confidential company data.

Review
This policy will be reviewed annually by the People Operations team and may be updated to reflect changes in business needs.
